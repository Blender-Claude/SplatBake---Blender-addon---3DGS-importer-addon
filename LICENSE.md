## About the names in the copyright line

Nothing in the GPL requires a legal name — a pseudonym or set of initials is
accepted, and copyright arises automatically on creation regardless of the
name used. Initials are, however, weaker if the licence ever has to be
enforced, because someone must be able to show that `MMJ` is you. The
copyright line above therefore carries the project's published home,
https://github.com/Blender-Claude, which ties the credit to an
account with a verifiable history rather than to two letters alone.

Note also that in several jurisdictions — the United States among them —
copyright protects human authorship, so naming an AI in the credit line is a
statement of how the work was made, not a grant of rights to the tool. The
enforceable rights sit with the human author.

This is general background, not legal advice; take a professional opinion if
anything commercial depends on it.

## Why GPL

Blender's Python API is GPL-licensed, and add-ons that import `bpy` are treated
by the Blender Foundation as derivative works, so they must ship under a
GPL-compatible license. GPL is therefore the safe and conventional choice here,
not merely a preference.

## Imported scenes are separately licensed

Splat captures you import carry their own terms. Several public scenes are
CC-BY, which requires crediting the original author in anything you publish.
That obligation belongs to the scene, not to this add-on.
